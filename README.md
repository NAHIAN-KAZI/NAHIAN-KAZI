## 👋 About Me

I design and build production AI/ML systems, with recent work centered on **Bangla-language voice AI** — conversational and outbound voice agents deployed for clients including 10 Minute School, City Bank, and bKash, closely tied to the **Verbex AI** voice platform.

On the research side, I work on **Bangla LLM evaluation**, including a preprint (*MicroDSPNet*) benchmarking LLMs across Bangladeshi legal datasets, and I'm broadly interested in AI for **linguistic and financial inclusion** in the Bengali-speaking world.

My broader background spans computer vision, document intelligence, and edge-optimized deep learning — from my undergraduate thesis on lightweight CNNs for waste classification to current work on scalable, production-ready ML pipelines.
